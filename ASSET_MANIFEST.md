# GEODIS Nashville Distribution Center Simulation — Master Asset Manifest

## Executive Overview
This manifest establishes the single source of truth for all visual assets across the **WarehousePro GEODIS Nashville Simulation Platform**. Every asset is anchored to authentic operational standards derived from GEODIS distribution centers in the Middle Tennessee logistics corridor (specifically the multi-client fulfillment facilities located along the **Park 840 East / Highway 109 corridor in Lebanon and Mt. Juliet, TN**).

### Facility & Operational Grounding Parameters
- **Illumination Architecture**: 5000K daylight-white commercial high-bay LED luminaires (Lithonia I-BEAM / Cree CXB industrial series) providing uniform 35–50 foot-candles at floor level to eliminate deep shadowing and glare on 1D/2D barcodes.
- **Surface & Concrete Standards**: Diamond-polished industrial slab treated with lithium silicate hardener/densifier (subtle specular floor bounce), marked with OSHA Safety Yellow (#FFCC00 / RAL 1023) 4-inch traffic boundaries and 45-degree yellow/black hazard hatching on pedestrian crosswalks.
- **Brand Identity**: Corporate GEODIS Blue (`#004A99` / Pantone 2935 C) applied to structural column collision bollards, rack upright post protectors, zone hanging placards, and logistics equipment.
- **Barcoding Protocols**: Standard GS1-128, SSCC-18, Code 128 Subset B/C for warehouse shelf location tags (ALOC) with high-contrast inverted check-digit boxes (Mod-43 / Mod-103), and standard 12-digit UPC-A (GTIN-12) product barcodes.
- **Hardware Profile**: Landscape wrist-worn RF terminal (Symbol / Zebra WT4090 / WT41N0 / WT6000) with 320×240 / 800×480 display coupled to dual-finger index ring scanner (Zebra RS5100 / RS6000) with green targeting reticle and ambidextrous thumb trigger.

## Technical Art Guidelines & Asset Specifications
1. **SVG Precision**: All vector files must include proper coordinate viewBoxes, inline linear/radial gradients simulating metallic aluminum, matte plastic, textured kraft cardboard, and high-contrast typography. No external font dependencies; all glyphs use standard monospace/sans-serif or outlined vector paths.
2. **5000K Lighting Norm**: Photorealistic assets and prompts specify 5000K cool white daylight LED overhead lighting, minimal directional shadow, and soft specular highlights typical of sealed concrete distribution centers.
3. **Barcoding Integrity**: All barcodes represent valid Code 128 / UPC-A symbology ratios with quiet zones at both margins and human-readable text underneath.
